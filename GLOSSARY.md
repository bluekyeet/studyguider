# StudyGuider

A personal study companion for NCEA and university study: it turns a learner's own material into lessons, reviews and practice exams, and tracks them against their real assessments.

## Language

### People

**Account**:
One learner's private space, holding all of their study data; an Administrator is an Account that can also manage other Accounts.
_Avoid_: User, login

### What is studied

**Programme**:
The NCEA programme or university degree that groups a learner's Subjects.
_Avoid_: Plan, degree, qualification

**Subject**:
Something the learner is enrolled in and studies, whether an NCEA subject at a level or a university paper.
_Avoid_: Paper, course, unit

**Topic**:
The unit of study within a Subject: for NCEA it is exactly one Standard; for a university Subject it is a learner-chosen part linked to one or more Learning outcomes.
_Avoid_: Unit, chapter, module

**Outcome**:
Something a Subject says the learner must be able to do; either a Standard or a Learning outcome.
_Avoid_: Objective, competency

**Standard**:
The NCEA kind of Outcome: a numbered achievement standard worth credits.
_Avoid_: AS, unit standard

**Learning outcome**:
The university kind of Outcome, as listed in a paper descriptor or course outline.
_Avoid_: LO, objective

**Learning topic**:
A Topic the learner is actively studying, with a goal, so it gets daily lessons.
_Avoid_: Course, enrolment

**Lesson**:
The day's teaching for one Learning topic, prepared overnight from its Sources.
_Avoid_: Class, module

**Learning phase**:
The normal Lesson phase, teaching a Learning topic step by step.

**Exam prep phase**:
The three weeks before an Assessment, when Lessons become mixed exam-style practice.
_Avoid_: Cram mode

**Maintenance phase**:
After an Assessment, when a Learning topic drops to a weekly Lesson.

**Practice section**:
A part of a Lesson where the learner's answers are saved and get AI feedback.
_Avoid_: Exercise, quiz

**Explanation**:
StudyGuider's answer to a learner's question about a section of a Lesson.
_Avoid_: Explain (as a noun)

**Note**:
Text the learner keeps on a Topic, written themselves or saved from an Explanation.
_Avoid_: Summary, memo

**Hint**:
A nudge on a question that does not give away the answer.
_Avoid_: Clue, tip

**Teaching method**:
The single app-wide way StudyGuider teaches, the same for every Subject.
_Avoid_: Subject method, pedagogy

**Teaching style**:
The learner's presentation preferences (tone, reading level and the like) for their Account, a Subject or a Topic.
_Avoid_: Style profile

### Material

**Resource**:
An item the learner uploaded to the Library (Past papers, Marking guides, slides, notes), kept with its extracted text.
_Avoid_: File, document, upload

**Source**:
A passage of a Resource that a Lesson, Explanation or Marking cites as evidence.
_Avoid_: Reference, citation

**Past paper**:
An original official exam or test document, NCEA or university, held as a Resource.
_Avoid_: Paper (alone), exam paper

**Marking guide**:
The answer guide to a Past paper, official or AI-written (and then labelled as such).
_Avoid_: Schedule, assessment schedule, answer key

### Assessment and practice

**Assessment**:
A real, dated, weighted event for a Subject (exam, test, internal or assignment) that has a Result.
_Avoid_: Exam, test (as separate terms)

**Exam**:
A sitting inside StudyGuider: a set of questions the learner answers and has marked, drawn from Past papers, mixed practice or unofficial AI practice.
_Avoid_: Test, quiz, paper

**Complete exam**:
An Exam with at least three questions, every one answered and marked.
_Avoid_: Full paper, paper

**AI practice**:
Questions or Exams StudyGuider wrote itself, never taken from a Past paper.
_Avoid_: Mock, generated paper

**Rapid questions**:
Fast exam questions on a Topic with no feedback until the Rapid session ends.
_Avoid_: Drill, flash questions

**Rapid session**:
One go at Rapid questions, marked in the background and reported as a Grade breakdown.

**Review**:
Scheduled spaced-repetition recall of questions drawn from completed lessons and wrong answers.
_Avoid_: Revision, flashcards

**Variant**:
A fresh rewording of a Review question that tests the same thing.
_Avoid_: Copy, clone

**Exam revision**:
A re-teaching pack for a Topic built from the learner's Rapid-question misses.
_Avoid_: Revision (alone), review

### Marking and progress

**Mark**:
What a question earns when marked, out of the marks it is worth.
_Avoid_: Score, points

**Grade**:
A band on a scale (NCEA N/A/M/E, or a university letter) derived from Marks.
_Avoid_: Level, rating

**Result**:
The real outcome the learner records against an Assessment.
_Avoid_: Grade, score

**Marking**:
StudyGuider's advisory AI judgement of one answer; never an official mark.
_Avoid_: Official mark, assessment

**Override**:
The learner's own correction of a Marking.
_Avoid_: Appeal, remark

**Unofficial**:
The label every AI output carries (Lessons, Marking, Grade estimates, AI practice): study help, not an NZQA or university result.
_Avoid_: Verified, approved

**Predicted recall**:
The estimated chance the learner would remember a Topic right now, from their Review history.
_Avoid_: Mastery, retention score

**Grade estimate**:
The likely Grade on a Subject or Standard, from full Past paper Exams or entered Results; never official.
_Avoid_: Predicted grade, mastery

### Rewards

**Points**:
The reward currency, earned only from work already recorded as correct and spent on cosmetics.
_Avoid_: Coins, currency, XP

**Pull**:
One gacha draw that spends Points for a random Item.
_Avoid_: Loot box, roll

**Item**:
A collectable cosmetic; it never affects study.
_Avoid_: Reward, prize

**Character**:
The learner's dressable figure shown on Leaderboards.
_Avoid_: Avatar

**Outfit**:
The Items the Character is wearing, one per slot.

**Showcase**:
Up to twelve Items the learner chooses to display.

**Leaderboard**:
A ranking of Accounts that have not hidden themselves, showing only username, picture, Character and counts.
